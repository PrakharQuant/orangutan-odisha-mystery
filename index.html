01 THE CASE

Something doesn't add up.

Orangutans are native to the rainforests of Southeast Asia. Yet five juveniles were found together in a coastal area of Odisha, India.

Officials are investigating how they arrived there. The current reporting points to multiple possibilities — including trafficking — but no definitive route or network has been established.

04 AGE FORENSICS

Why does their age matter?

Because a six-month-old orangutan is not a traveller.

~6–8 MONTHS

The reported age

At this stage, an orangutan infant is still heavily dependent on its mother.

YEARS

Maternal dependence

Orangutan mothers and young remain closely associated for years while the youngster learns to survive.

7–9 YEARS

Long reproductive interval

Female orangutans typically reproduce only after long intervals, making five similarly aged infants from one mother implausible.

🔎
DETECTIVE'S NOTE

One mother or several?

Five infants of roughly similar age are extremely unlikely to be siblings from a single mother. That makes the question of how these five individuals came together particularly important.
